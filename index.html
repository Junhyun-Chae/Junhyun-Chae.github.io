Master's research at the University of Hyogo, supervised by Prof. Hiroyasu Inoue.

Journal article First author Open access

Multilayer Propagation of Cross-Country Systemic Risk

Junhyun Chae, Hiroyasu Inoue · Journal of Risk and Financial Management 19(3), 197 · March 2026

A model that couples cross-border bank lending with international trade for 20 economies, 2000–2023. Looking at both layers changes which countries appear systemically important.

Read the paper Code

How the model works

  1. Two layers for 20 economies: bank lending (BIS) and trade (UN Comtrade).
  2. A shock spreads within a layer by DebtRank.
  3. Once distress passes a threshold, it jumps to the other layer.
  4. Counting both layers reshuffles which countries look risky.
