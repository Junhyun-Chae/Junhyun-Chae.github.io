About

I work on the digital asset team at InterTrade (TSE: 3747) in Tokyo. I designed and built, from scratch, a PKCS#11 signing adapter that connects an institutional MPC custody platform to hardware security modules, and I automate token issuance and redemption on a public blockchain using AWS. I am interested in where the two meet: understanding risk in financial networks, and building digital asset systems that stay secure when things go wrong.

Research

Journal article · first author

Multilayer Propagation of Cross-Country Systemic Risk

Junhyun Chae, Hiroyasu Inoue · Journal of Risk and Financial Management 19(3), 197 · March 2026

We model cross-border contagion on two coupled layers, international bank lending (BIS Locational Banking Statistics) and bilateral trade (UN Comtrade), for 20 economies from 2000 to 2023. Shocks spread within each layer by DebtRank and cross to the other layer when distress passes a threshold. Accounting for both layers changes which countries look systemically important, and the rankings hold across a wide range of thresholds.
